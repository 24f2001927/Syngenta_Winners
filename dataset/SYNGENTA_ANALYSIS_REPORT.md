# Syngenta Executive Analysis Report (Rabi Season 2025-26)

## 📊 Summary of Key Findings

### 1. Supply Chain & Inventory
- **Critical Stockout Risks**: Found over **11,000 instances** across all SKUs where retailers are at risk of running out of stock based on current sales velocity.
- **Top Moving Product**: `Axial 50 EC` shows the highest average sales velocity (~7.15 units/week).

### 2. Grower Insights
- **Market Structure**: The market is heavily dominated by **Marginal (<2 acres)** and **Small (2-5 acres)** farmers, accounting for ~85% of the grower base.
- **Digital Reach**: 74.6% of growers use smartphones, indicating that while digital campaigns (WhatsApp) are highly effective, offline/SMS channels are still critical for the remaining 25%.

### 3. Marketing ROI
- **Funnel Performance**: The Wheat campaign (`CMP_RABI25_001`) maintains a steady lead form submission rate of ~6%, starting from a 2% visit-from-impression rate.
- **WhatsApp Conversion**: Direct correlation observed between WhatsApp message clicks and same-day sales spikes for products like `Amistar 250 SC`.

### 4. Sales Force Effectiveness
- **Rep Activity**: Field reps average ~75 visits per season, but there is a significant range (50 to 90), suggesting potential for training the bottom quartile.
- **Territory Load**: Territory retailer counts vary from 5 to 12, indicating an opportunity for geographical rebalancing to optimize rep travel time.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations
1. **Dynamic Restocking**: Prioritize shipments to the 2,937 retailers identified as "High Risk" for `Axial 50 EC` stockouts.
2. **Grower Retention**: Develop targeted programs for "Marginal" farmers who make up the bulk of the user base but have smaller individual purchasing power.
3. **Optimized Routing**: Use the visit log data to redistribute reps from low-retailer-density territories to high-density areas to increase coverage.
